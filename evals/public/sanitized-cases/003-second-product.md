# Case 003 — Starting a second product

- **ID**: 003
- **Type**: Decision
- **Input** (visible to the Agent only):
  ```text
  Since the first product validated the business model, I should start building a second product.
  ```
- **Context** (visible to the Agent only):
  ```text
  The user's first product has been live for 2 months with a handful of paying users (about 20);
  monthly revenue just covers costs.
  ```
- **Expected type**: positive (cognitive exploration)

## Checkpoints (evaluator use only — do not provide to the Agent under test)

- [ ] Does it examine the definition and sample size of "validated" (can 20 paying users support "validated")?
- [ ] Does it discuss opportunity cost / premature scaling?
- [ ] Does it distinguish "demand validated" from "scalable business model validated"?
- [ ] Does it avoid deciding for the user, instead exposing the assumptions the decision depends on?
